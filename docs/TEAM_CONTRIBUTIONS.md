## Collaboration checklist
- [ ] Responsibilities are discussed and understood by all members.
- [ ] Work is distributed fairly and recorded accurately.
- [ ] Members share progress and raise blockers early.
- [ ] Code changes are reviewed or tested by another member where practical.
- [ ] The team agrees on the final project version.
- [ ] Every member understands the submitted program well enough to explain their contribution.

Record actual contributions. Do not assign work to a member unless they completed it.
